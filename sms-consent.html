How owners opt in

01

Who opts in

Only the owner or staff of a business Hikmon builds a website for. These are our own clients — never the general public, and never purchased lists.

02

How they opt in

During onboarding, the owner enters their own mobile number and checks an explicit consent box on the form below (or on their account setup page). Consent is express, written, and unchecked by default.

03

What they receive

Transactional lead-alert notifications only — triggered when a customer submits a form on their website. No marketing, no promotions, no third-party content.

Privacy & data use

Mobile numbers collected for Hikmon Lead Alerts are used solely to deliver the lead-alert notifications described above. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile information or consent with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile opt-in data is shared with anyone except the SMS provider strictly to transmit the messages. Owners can revoke consent at any time by replying STOP or emailing kareem@hikmon.net.
