Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

This explains how localservicemarketers.com uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They help the site work properly and help us understand how it's used.

How we use them

  • Essential — needed for the site and booking page to function (e.g. keeping your booking session working). These can't be switched off.
  • Analytics — anonymous, aggregated usage data so we can improve the site. These don't identify you personally.

We don't use cookies to sell your data or build advertising profiles.

Third parties

Some providers we use may set their own cookies when their features load — for example our host (Vercel), our payment processor (Stripe) on the checkout page, and embedded fonts (Google Fonts).

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may break parts of the site, including booking.
